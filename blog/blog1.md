# Blog 1 
What is Data Science to Me?

Data Science matters because it is used in my everyday life, as soon as I wake up all the way until before I go to sleep.
Learning data science in social and interdisciplinary settings matters because data doesn't exist in isolation. It is gathered from individuals, groups, communities, and surroundings, and the approach we investigate it might impact actual decisions. Knowing the societal background of data enables us to pose improved questions, detect prejudice, and critically consider who is included or left out.

An interdisciplinary approach is also useful as many real-world challenges cannot be fully appreciated through data science alone. Public health, education, climate change, finance, and social injustice need knowledge from several disciplines. While disciplines such as sociology, psychology, ethics, and political science assist in elucidating what those patterns imply and how they ought to be utilized, data science offers tools for discovering patterns and evidence.

Learning data science in these settings finally cultivates more than simply technical competencies. It promotes responsible decision-making, teamwork, and sensitivity to the human repercussions of data-driven decisions. It helps us to see data as information related to people and society rather than as just numbers
